### What Drives Me

I'm driven by a simple curiosity: I want to understand how things work and why they work the way they do.

My approach to learning is taking things apart—breaking down complex mechanisms, focusing on individual components in isolation, and seeing how each piece interacts systematically to work toward a larger whole.

A big part of that is understanding the everyday technology we take for granted and trying to recreate it from scratch from first principles, relying on open tools and resourcefulness.
